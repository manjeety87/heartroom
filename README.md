# Heartroom

Private game rooms for friends, with voice. Open a room, share the code, and play notebook-style
games together while you talk.

**Launch games**
- **Bingo 1–25.** Fill your 5×5 grid, take turns calling numbers, and complete 5 lines to spell B‑I‑N‑G‑O.
  2–6 players, custom grids from 3×3 up to 8×8, and a vs Computer mode.
- **Word Guess.** Set a secret word for your rival and guess theirs, with right-place / wrong-place
  feedback.
